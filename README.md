# Sun & Moon — Pixel Art Game

A Unity 2D cooperative adventure. Sun and Moon share one keyboard, hold crossings for each other, gather keepsakes, and reach home together across ten chapters.

## Controls

| Partner | Move | Jump |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sun | A / D | Hold W for a full leap, tap for a shorter hop |
| Moon | Left / Right | Hold Up for a full leap, tap for a shorter hop |

Also: `R` restarts the chapter, `P` / `Esc` pauses, and `Esc` on a finished chapter opens the chapter map.

## Play

Open the project in Unity 6000.3 and load `Assets/Scenes/SunAndMoon.unity`. Progress unlocks and keepsake scores save with `PlayerPrefs` on this machine or browser.

In Play Mode, run **Sun & Moon / Validate Gameplay (Play Mode)** to exercise chapter rules, crossings, the lift step, jump height, fade warnings, and shared exits.
